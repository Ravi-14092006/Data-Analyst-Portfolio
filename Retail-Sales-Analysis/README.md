## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses need to identify high-performing products, profitable customer segments, regional sales trends, and customer purchasing behavior to improve decision-making.

## 🛠 Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX
* Excel
* SQL

## 📈 Key Insights

* 10,000 retail transactions analyzed
* Revenue and profit KPI dashboard
* Monthly revenue trend analysis
* Region and payment method comparison
* Top 10 products by revenue
* Customer drill-through detail page
